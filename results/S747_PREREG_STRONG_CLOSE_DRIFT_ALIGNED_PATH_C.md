## ۱) قاعدهٔ منجمد رخداد (پیش از دیدن هر عدد)

```
ATR100 = atr_plain(h,l,c,100)  (causal)
rng    = high − low
clv    = (close − low) / rng
DRIFT  = SMA(close, W_D) با shift(1)  (causal)

LONG : rng ≥ K_R·ATR100  AND  clv ≥ CLV_K      AND close > open AND close > DRIFT
SHORT: rng ≥ K_R·ATR100  AND  (1−clv) ≥ CLV_K  AND close < open AND close < DRIFT
ورود: open میلهٔ بعد · SL = K_SL·ATR100 · TP = max(RR·SL, SL) · HOLD=16 · allow_overlap=False
```
